Questions, answered

Isn't this what Alibaba does? Alibaba lists suppliers and checks their paperwork. We visit and film each factory ourselves, keep their samples in Australia, and lock your order to the sample you held.

How do you make money? Two ways, both on this page: your membership, and a 5% commission each factory pays us on the orders it gets through us. Factory prices here are the ones they signed for all members.

Where are the samples kept? In our two hubs, Brisbane and Sydney. Each checks every unit against the sealed master, and the hub nearest you with stock sends yours.

Who films the factories? Our own team, in person, holding a dated board with the supplier's ID. We re-film every factory each year.

Why can't I see a supplier's name? So orders come through us, where the inspection and the spec lock protect you. Names and contacts open with your first order.

What if the bulk goods don't match the sample? The supplier reworks them at its own cost and they're inspected again. If they fail twice, the order is cancelled and your deposit is refunded.

Who is the importer? You are. Our customs broker partner handles the declaration and quotes freight, duty and GST before you pay anything.

Will I pay duty? Usually not on goods made in China, as long as they come with a ChAFTA Certificate of Origin, which our suppliers provide. GST of 10% still applies at the border.

Do I need an ABN? Yes. Membership is for businesses, and the free sample is tied to your ABN.

Can I keep the sample? Yes, it's yours. It's marked as a sample and can't be resold.

What happens after three months? Membership becomes A$45 a month. We remind you a week before, and you can cancel from your account.

Can I ask for a change to a product? Yes. Choose "Need a change" on your sample; we ask the supplier for a revised spec and a new sample.

How fast is a bulk order? Most suppliers quote 25 to 40 days to make the goods, then about three weeks by sea.
