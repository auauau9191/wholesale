What is Lotwise?

A wholesale membership that works on both sides of the trade. Our team visits and films Chinese factories. Their samples sit on our shelves in Brisbane and Sydney. You hold one, then order a lot that's inspected against it before it sails. A$60 for three months, then A$45 a month. No fee on your orders.

What happens on each side?

Most importers only see their own side. Here's both, at the same moment, from the first visit to your door.

Questions, answered

How is this different from Alibaba?

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.

Who films the factories?

Our own filmer, on the visit that gets a factory listed. The board in every clip shows the supplier code and the date it was filmed.

Do I need an ABN?

Yes. We check it against the register, then activate your account within one business day.

Can I change colour or packaging?

Yes, through us. Changes are quoted by the factory and go into a new spec revision, signed before production starts.
