How does an order work?

You hold the sample first. Nothing ships until the goods match it.

  1. See the factory on our film.Gate, line, QC bench and packing, with the supplier code and date on a board. Under 70 out of 100, it isn't listed.
  2. Hold the sample in Australia.From Brisbane or Sydney within 2 business days. 14 days to say yes, no or change it.
  3. Order against that sample.Locked to the unit you held and its signed spec. 30% starts production.
  4. Inspected before it sails.Compared with the sealed master. The factory gets the other 70% only when it matches.

Questions, answered

How is this different from Alibaba?

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.

Where do these numbers come from?

The factory's price for that quantity, converted at our current rate, plus estimated sea freight, duty, our broker's fee shared across your lot, and GST. Every product page shows the same sheet.

Do I need an ABN?

Yes. We check it against the register, then activate your account within one business day.
