Questions, answered

How is this different from Alibaba?

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.

What if the goods don't match?

They don't ship. The factory reworks, and the inspection is booked again before the balance falls due.

Do I pay import duty?

Often not. With a ChAFTA origin certificate goods enter duty free; without one, duty is about 5% of the goods value.

Do I need an ABN?

Yes. We check it, then activate your account within one business day.
