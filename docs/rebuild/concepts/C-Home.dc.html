What is Lotwise?

A membership for Australian businesses that buy from Chinese factories. You see the factory on our film, take the sample off our shelf, and order a lot that's inspected against it. A$60 for your first three months, then A$45 a month. No fee on your orders.

How does an order work?

  1. 1

    See the factory on our film

    Dated, at the gate, line, QC bench and packing. Under 70 out of 100 isn't listed.

  2. 2

    Take the sample off our shelf

    Sent within 2 business days. You have 14 days to say yes, no or change it.

  3. 3

    Order the lot

    Locked to the unit you held and its signed spec. 30% starts production.

  4. 4

    Checked before it sails

    Matched to the sealed master on our shelf, then the factory is paid the rest.

Where are the samples?

On shelves at our two hubs. Each unit is tagged and checked against the sealed master when it lands.

What does it cost?

Factories pay us 5% from their side, so there's no fee on your orders.

How is this different from Alibaba?

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.
