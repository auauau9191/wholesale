What is Lotwise?

A membership for Australian businesses that buy from Chinese factories. Every listed factory has been visited and filmed by our team, every product has a sample on a shelf here, and every order is inspected against that sample. A$60 for three months, then A$45 a month. No fee on your orders.

What do we film on a visit?

Four places, in this order, with the supplier code and the date on the board. We score the factory out of 100, and anything under 70 isn't listed.

Then what happens after I hold the sample?

You order against it. Your order is locked to that unit and its signed spec, you pay 30% to start production, and the factory gets the rest only after an inspector matches the cartons to the sealed master on our shelf.
