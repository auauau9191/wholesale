What is Lotwise?

A membership for Australian businesses that buy from Chinese factories. You see each factory on our film, hold its sample here, then order a lot matched to that sample. A$60 for your first three months, then A$45 a month. No fee on your orders.

How does an order work?

Four steps, and you can stop after any of them. Nothing ships until it matches the sample you held.

  1. 1

    See the factory on our film

    Our filmer holds a board with the supplier code and date at the gate, on the line, at the QC bench and in packing. Under 70 out of 100, a factory isn't listed.

  2. 2

    Hold the sample here

    Every product has units on a shelf in Brisbane or Sydney. Yours leaves within 2 business days, and you have 14 days to say yes, no or change it.

  3. 3

    Order against that sample

    Your order is locked to the unit you held and its signed spec. You pay 30% to start production.

  4. 4

    Inspected before it sails

    An inspector compares the cartons with the sealed master. The factory gets the other 70% only when they match.

What if the bulk goods don't match the sample?

They don't ship.

A master unit of every product is sealed with the factory and kept on our shelf. The inspector opens your cartons before they sail and compares them with it. If anything is out, the factory reworks and the inspection is booked again before the balance falls due.

Questions, answered

How is this different from Alibaba?

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.

Do I pay import duty?

Often not. Chinese goods with a ChAFTA origin certificate enter Australia duty free; without one, duty is about 5% of the goods value.

Why can't I see a supplier's name?

You see the film, the score and every price. The legal name and contacts open once your order is placed through us, so the inspection and the spec lock cover you.

Do I need an ABN?

Yes. We check it against the register, then activate your account within one business day.
