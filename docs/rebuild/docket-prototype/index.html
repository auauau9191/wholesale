What if the bulk goods don't match?

They don't ship. The inspector opens your cartons and compares them with the master sealed on our shelf. If anything is out, the factory reworks and the inspection is booked again before the balance falls due.

Questions, answered

How is this different from Alibaba?+

Alibaba is a directory: you find a factory, then carry the risk yourself. We visit the factory, film it, hold a sample in Australia, and lock your order to that sample.

Where do the docket's numbers come from?+

The factory's price for that quantity, converted at our current rate, plus estimated sea freight, duty, our broker's fee shared across your lot, and GST. Members see the same docket on every product.

Why can't I see a supplier's name?+

You see the film, the score and every price. The supplier's legal name and contacts open once your order is placed through us, so the inspection and the spec lock cover you.

Do I pay import duty?+

Often not. Chinese goods with a ChAFTA origin certificate enter Australia duty free; without one, duty is about 5% of the goods value. Each docket shows which applies.

Do I need an ABN?+

Yes. We check it against the register, then activate your account within one business day.
