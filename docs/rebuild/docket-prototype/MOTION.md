# Docket: design and motion system (prototype)

Live prototype: Claude artifact "Lotwise Docket". Source: `index.html` here. Lottie files are generated by `lottie_gen.py`.

## Colour
Every colour has one job.

| Token | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Carbon | The ground. Carbon paper is the copy underneath every docket. |
| Paper | Thermal docket paper. Everything Lotwise states as fact is printed on it. |
| Print | Thermal black. |
| Red | The printer's second colour. Used only for totals and the one action on a screen. |
| Pen blue | A person at Lotwise checked this: ticks, rings, stamps, hand notes. |

## Type
Martian Mono only, from hairline to black.

| Role | Setting |
|---|---|
| Headlines | 75% width, weight 800 |
| Big figures | 75% width, weight 150 |
| Reading text | 75% width, weight 400 |

## Motion grammar
Every animation belongs to one actor, and each actor always moves the same way.

| Actor | What moves | How it moves |
|---|---|---|
| The machine prints | Dockets feed out of a slot in line steps. A changed figure reprints. Page headings print as you reach them. The next page feeds in. | Stepped and linear, 64 ms a line. |
| Our team marks | Proof ticks, the ring around a total, the "Matched to master" and "Requested" stamps, step ticks. These are Lottie animations, always pen blue. | Eased like a hand. |
| You tear | Choosing a product tears the old docket off. The Join ticket and the sample slip tear along their perforation. Leaving a page tears it away. | Springy. |

### Supporting motion
These all serve the three actors above:
- **Parallax:** carbon copies sit deeper than the docket, and step photos drift behind the paper.
- **Scroll roll:** shows how much of the page is left.
- **3D sample:** dragged by you. When you first see it, our team places it on the shelf, so it eases in with no bounce.

### Reduced motion
With reduced motion on, nothing moves. Every mark rests on its final frame, and the 3D sample stops idling but can still be turned.
